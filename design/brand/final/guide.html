App icons

Full-bleed squares; iOS and Android round the corners. Files in design/brand/final/, re-export with node scripts/export-icons.mjs.

Morphy

The mascot is a drop of aurora light. Morphy reacts, never talks, and never covers a button. In the app now: idle on Home, catching while a file is dragged over, a happy hop when it's done, a wobble on errors.

Voice

  • Short and warm. "Drop anything", not "Upload your media files".
  • Errors say what to do. "That file has no sound. Try a video with audio in it."
  • Private by default, say it plainly. "Files never leave your device."
  • Lowercase brand name. Write "morph" in text, "morph." in the logo.
